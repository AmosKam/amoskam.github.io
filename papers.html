  • 2026
    Condensed Brown Comenetz Duality
    with Roey Hel-Or

    Constructs an involutive duality on condensed spectra with locally compact homotopy groups, extending Pontryagin duality to spectra. Dualization induces Pontryagin duality on homotopy groups with degrees reversed. In particular, every ordinary spectrum, viewed as a discrete condensed spectrum, satisfies biduality without finiteness assumptions.

    Local structure of smooth p-adic analytic Artin stacks

    Resolved Clausen’s Conjecture 5.17 on the local light–profinite structure of smooth For p-adic analytic Artin stacks, the proof includes a generalization of the classical van Dantzig theorem to the case of groupoids and a closed-skeleton reduction. In particular, this result implies that smooth p-adic analytic Artin stacks are !-good.
